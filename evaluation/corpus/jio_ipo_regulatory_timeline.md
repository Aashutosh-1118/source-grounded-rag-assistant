# Jio Platforms IPO: regulatory steps and timeline (research notes)

These notes were prepared on 1 November 2026 from public news articles, written in our own words. Sources are listed at the end.

## Draft prospectus

Jio Platforms filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) with SEBI on 19 June 2026. The filing came a few hours after the company's board approved it, and on the same day as the annual general meeting of Reliance Industries. The draft described a book-built fresh issue of up to 27 crore equity shares of Rs 10 each, with no offer for sale. The objects of the issue were to prepay certain borrowings of Reliance Jio Infocomm and to meet general corporate purposes. Reliance Industries held 593.78 crore shares in Jio Platforms as of 31 March 2026.

## Early valuation talk

When the draft was filed, a Bloomberg report said investment bankers had proposed a valuation of $130 billion to $170 billion. The final valuation was only set closer to launch. At the IPO price, the company was worth about US$108 billion according to later market reports.

## SEBI observation letter

Jio Platforms received SEBI's observation letter on 28 August 2026, and Reliance Industries disclosed this in a stock exchange filing on the same day. SEBI's observations alone do not mean the IPO has been launched. The company still had to complete further steps and file its final offer document before the issue could open. At that stage the expected issue size was around Rs 37,700 crore, and the plan was that up to 50 percent would go to QIBs and at least 35 percent to retail investors.

## Reliance Industries at that time

For context, Reliance Industries reported consolidated net profit of Rs 23,196 crore for the first quarter of FY27, up 6.12 percent from a year earlier. Its gross revenue for the quarter was Rs 3,40,257 crore, up 24.50 percent. Its share price was Rs 1,287 on 28 August 2026.

## From filing to listing

The IPO went from the DRHP on 19 June 2026 to the SEBI observation letter on 28 August 2026, then to the anchor book on 19 October 2026, public bidding from 21 to 23 October 2026 and listing on 28 October 2026.
