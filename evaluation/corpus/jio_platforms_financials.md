# Jio Platforms: financials, valuation and shareholding (research notes)

These notes were prepared on 1 November 2026 from the company's IPO filings as reported by news and IPO data websites, written in our own words. Amounts are in Rs crore unless stated. Sources are listed at the end.

## Financial results

- FY24: revenue from operations Rs 1,09,558.10 crore, EBITDA Rs 54,958.70 crore, profit before tax Rs 28,808.00 crore, profit after tax Rs 21,423.20 crore.
- FY25: revenue from operations Rs 1,28,218.40 crore, EBITDA Rs 64,170.00 crore, profit before tax Rs 35,127.30 crore, profit after tax Rs 26,109.00 crore.
- FY26: revenue from operations Rs 1,46,885.30 crore, EBITDA Rs 76,255.40 crore, profit before tax Rs 40,353.10 crore, profit after tax Rs 30,049.10 crore.

## Growth and margins

In FY26 revenue grew 14.6 percent over FY25 and EBITDA grew 18.8 percent. Profit before tax rose 14.9 percent and profit after tax rose 15.1 percent. The EBITDA margin improved to 51.91 percent in FY26 from 50.05 percent in FY25 and 50.16 percent in FY24. Net cash from operations was Rs 77,556.30 crore in FY26, and cash and equivalents stood at Rs 16,557.00 crore at the end of FY26. Return on net worth was 9.42 percent in FY26.

## Valuation at the issue price

At the issue price of Rs 1,119, the price to earnings ratio was 33.28x on FY26 earnings, and diluted earnings per share were Rs 33.59. Net asset value per share was Rs 373.66 for FY26. The implied market capitalisation at the issue price was Rs 10,00,277.55 crore before the IPO and Rs 10,30,490.55 crore after it.

## Business snapshot

Jio Platforms was incorporated on 15 November 2019 in Ahmedabad. RJIL served 533.3 million customers as of 30 June 2026, and the wider Jio Platforms customer base was 524.4 million at the end of FY26 compared with 488.2 million a year before. Average revenue per user (ARPU) was Rs 215.6 per month in the three months ended 30 June 2026. Prepaid connectivity made up 77.08 percent of revenue from operations in FY26.

## Shareholding

Reliance Industries held 66.43 percent of Jio Platforms before the IPO, and its stake falls to 64.48 percent after the new shares are issued. Other large holders include Jaadhu Holdings, an affiliate of Meta, with 9.98 percent, and Google International with 7.73 percent.
