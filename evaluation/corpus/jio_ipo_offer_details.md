# Jio Platforms IPO: offer details (research notes)

These notes were prepared on 1 November 2026 from public news articles and IPO data websites, written in our own words. Figures are as reported. Some data sites differ slightly from each other, and where that matters it is mentioned. Sources are listed at the end.

## Offer structure

Jio Platforms Ltd is a subsidiary of Reliance Industries. Its IPO was a 100 percent book-built issue made up entirely of fresh shares: up to 27 crore equity shares with a face value of Rs 10 each. There was no offer for sale, so existing shareholders did not sell any shares in the issue. The shares are listed on NSE and BSE.

## Key dates

- Anchor bidding: 19 October 2026
- Issue opened: 21 October 2026
- Issue closed: 23 October 2026
- Basis of allotment: 26 October 2026
- Refunds and share credit: 27 October 2026
- Listing: 28 October 2026

## Price band and lot size

The price band was Rs 1,065 to Rs 1,119 per share. The issue price was fixed at the top of the band, at Rs 1,119 per share. One lot had 12 shares, so the minimum retail investment at the top of the band was Rs 13,428. Retail investors could bid for 1 to 14 lots, which meant between Rs 13,428 and Rs 1,87,992.

## Issue size

The issue size was reported at about Rs 30,200 crore. One data site gave Rs 30,213 crore and another gave Rs 30,188 crore. Earlier, in August 2026, the expected size had been around Rs 37,700 crore, before the price band was set. News outlets described it as India's biggest-ever IPO.

## Investor categories and reservations

Of the net issue, not more than 50 percent was for qualified institutional buyers (QIBs), not less than 15 percent for non-institutional investors (NIIs) and not less than 35 percent for retail investors.

Two special reservations existed. Eligible shareholders of Reliance Industries had a reserved portion of up to Rs 2,500 crore, with bids up to Rs 2 lakh and a record date of 14 October 2026. Employees had a reserved portion of up to Rs 500 crore, with bids up to Rs 5 lakh and a discount of Rs 54 per share.

## Anchor investors

The anchor book raised about Rs 8,109.85 crore. The five largest anchors were LIC with Rs 499.98 crore, New World Fund Inc with Rs 486.27 crore, the Government of Singapore with Rs 340.93 crore, SBI Focused Fund with Rs 315.00 crore and BNP Paribas Financial Markets ODI with Rs 303.25 crore. Half of the anchor shares are locked in until 24 November 2026 and the rest until 23 January 2027.

## Use of proceeds

About Rs 27,500 crore is meant to repay or prepay borrowings of Reliance Jio Infocomm Ltd (RJIL), a material subsidiary. Another Rs 2,217.90 crore is for general corporate purposes and Rs 469.70 crore covers issue expenses, which adds up to roughly Rs 30,187.60 crore.

## Managers and registrar

Kotak Mahindra Capital Company is named as the book running lead manager on the main IPO pages, and the offer had 19 book-running lead managers in total, including Morgan Stanley India, BofA Securities India, Axis Capital and ICICI Securities. The registrar is KFin Technologies Ltd.
