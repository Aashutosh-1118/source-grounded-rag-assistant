# Jio Platforms IPO: subscription and listing day (research notes)

These notes were prepared on 1 November 2026 from public news articles and IPO data websites, written in our own words. Subscription numbers change through the bidding window and differ a little between data sites, so the figures below are the final-day numbers from one data site. Sources are listed at the end.

## Subscription

The IPO closed on 23 October 2026 and was subscribed about 21 times overall. One data site reported the total at 21.36x late on the final day, and another site showed a slightly higher 22.19x. Overall subscription by day was 3.17x on day 1, 5.54x on day 2 and 21.36x on the final day.

By category, QIB 67.15x, NII 13.99x, retail 2.76x, employee 2.91x and the Reliance shareholder reservation 7.59x. Institutions drove the demand, while retail investors subscribed far less than institutions did.

Total bids were about 4.22 billion shares (4,21,87,71,852) against 19,75,25,934 shares on offer, not counting the anchor portion.

## Listing day: 28 October 2026

Jio Platforms listed on Wednesday, 28 October 2026. On NSE the shares listed at Rs 1,358, which was Rs 239 or 21.36 percent above the issue price of Rs 1,119. By about 10:47 AM the stock was trading near Rs 1,358.50 with an intraday high of Rs 1,375. Later in the session the NSE price reached about Rs 1,417.50, roughly 26.7 percent above the issue price, and news reports described a gain of nearly 27 percent on debut.

At the listing price the company's market capitalisation was about Rs 12,51,047 crore. For a retail investor who bought one lot at Rs 1,119, the Rs 13,428 investment was worth Rs 16,296 at the listing price, a gain of Rs 2,868 before charges and taxes.

## After the debut

On Thursday, 29 October 2026 the shares rose by as much as 5.1 percent. Jio Platforms added almost US$36 billion in market value over its first two trading sessions. Its market value was about US$144 billion, compared with about US$108 billion at the IPO price, which made it the second-most valuable listed company in India. Reports also noted a customer base of more than 500 million.
