# Home Loan Primer (sample document)

This is a synthetic sample written for testing a retrieval system. The numbers are invented.

## Fixed and floating rates
A fixed rate loan keeps the same interest rate for the whole term. A floating rate loan changes whenever the lender's benchmark rate changes. In the sample market, the average 30-year fixed rate was 6.8 percent in March, and the average 15-year fixed rate was 6.1 percent.

## Down payments
Most lenders in the sample market ask for a down payment of at least 5 percent of the purchase price. Borrowers who put down less than 20 percent usually pay private mortgage insurance, which costs between 0.5 and 1.5 percent of the loan amount per year.

## Credit scores
A credit score of 740 or higher qualifies for the lowest advertised rates. Borrowers with scores below 620 often need a government-backed loan program, and the minimum down payment for that program is 3.5 percent.

## Closing costs
Closing costs usually run between 2 and 5 percent of the loan amount. They include appraisal fees, title insurance and lender origination fees. The typical appraisal fee in the sample market is 450 dollars.

## Prepayment
Some loans charge a prepayment penalty if the borrower pays off the loan within the first 3 years. The penalty in the sample market is 2 percent of the remaining balance. Loans without a penalty usually carry a slightly higher rate.
